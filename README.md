An entry for the make.fun competition `u-deranalabs-six-words-one-story-f982e410`.

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network) writes one complete
story in exactly six words to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default
`/output/entry.txt`):

> He survived the war. Not peace.

A whole life in six words: a man comes home from the war, and the
peace he survived for is what finally takes him. Every word earns its
place — the turn lands on the last two, and the reader is left to fill
in how peace undid him.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
